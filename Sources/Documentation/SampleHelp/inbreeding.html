Nonrandom Mating

Inbreeding does not change allele frequencies; it changes how those alleles are packaged into genotypes.

Selfing

Each generation of selfing halves the frequency of heterozygotes, so the inbreeding coefficient F rises toward one.

Wahlund Effects

Pooling individuals from several demes produces a deficit of heterozygotes even when every deme is in Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium.
